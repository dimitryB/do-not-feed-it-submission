# Quotes

Do Not Feed It talks back in the words of Hemi's own livestreams — Midweek
with Max and the Engineering Office Hours, September and October 2026. Every
line below is from the stream's auto-captions, with stutters removed and
nothing else changed. Timestamps are minutes:seconds into the stream (past the
hour the minutes keep counting, so 1:00:56 is 60:56). This is a fan-made
contest entry; nothing here implies Hemi made or approved it.

The words live in `lib/render/lore.ts`. Captions never name anyone; a host's
name appears on screen only in a verse's citation.
